Placement Policy

Welcome to the Placement Policy page. Please read the guidelines below carefully to understand the rules for participating in the placement process.

1. Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet the following criteria:

  • CGPA of 7.0 or higher.
  • No academic backlogs.
  • Completion of required training.

2. Job Categories

  • Mass Recruitment: Open to all students.
  • Core: For specialized positions.
  • Dream: High-paying roles. Once placed in this category, students cannot apply elsewhere.

3. Application Limits

Students can apply to up to two companies at a time. After placement, further applications are restricted.

4. Off-Campus Jobs

Off-campus job applications are permitted. However, once placed through an on-campus recruitment, students are ineligible for off-campus positions.

5. Placement Rules

  • Students placed in a higher category cannot apply for lower ones.
  • After two job offers, students are automatically withdrawn from further placement processes.

6. Code of Conduct

Professional behavior is expected during interviews. Misconduct may result in disqualification from the placement process.
